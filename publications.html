2026
Journal article · Tectonophysics 928, 231158

Contribution of 2-D and 3-D magnetotelluric models to understand the tectonic evolution of the Dom Feliciano belt and adjacent basins, southern Brazil and northeastern Uruguay

A.M.C. Menezes; F. Chemale Jr.; P.P. Lugão; M.J. Unsworth; J. González Garcia; M. Cruz; S.B.A. Rolim; R.P. Phillipp; B. Kriegshäuser

In plain language

The study uses two- and three-dimensional MT models to investigate the structure and geological evolution of a belt and its neighbouring basins.

Why a customer might read it

For a customer, the useful lesson is how electrical models can contribute to a regional geological explanation. This is research in Brazil and Uruguay, not evidence of a Canadian resource discovery.

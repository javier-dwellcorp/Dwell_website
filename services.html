SERVICES

From what is known
to what comes next.

Bring us an early idea, a collection of historical data or a project ready for its next phase. You can enter the process at the point that fits your needs.

01

Preliminary tabletop consultation

Define the focus before expanding the scope.

Review the project purpose, location, available information and decision timeline. Identify the key questions and recommend a proportionate next step.

You receive: A focused consultation summary, initial information gaps and an outline for follow-up.

Learn more ↗
02

Existing data & opportunity assessment

Find the value in information already available.

Search public records, geological maps, historical reports, surveys and client-owned records. Check location, quality, coverage and relevance.

You receive: A data inventory, coverage assessment, preliminary interpretation and ranked gaps.

Learn more ↗
03

Data sourcing, purchasing & brokerage

Get the right missing information.

Identify commercial datasets, compare their usefulness and coordinate access with the data owners. We help make the data choice understandable before purchase.

You receive: Recommended datasets, their intended role, and the costs and usage terms to agree.

Learn more ↗
04

Survey design & new data acquisition

Measure what will make a difference.

Design ground investigations around the geological question. Establish station locations, access requirements and quality checks. Coordinate complementary specialists where appropriate.

You receive: A survey plan, field measurements and documented quality review.

Learn more ↗
05

Processing, modelling & interpretation

Turn measurements into geological understanding.

Clean and assess the data, construct models and compare the results with the rest of the evidence. Investigate alternatives instead of relying on a single attractive picture.

You receive: Maps, sections and models with assumptions, confidence and limitations explained.

Learn more ↗
06

Field Development Planning

Connect subsurface evidence with the development sequence.

Compare conceptual locations and phases, identify dependencies and plan further investigation with the project team.

You receive: Development options, investigation priorities, decision points and a staged roadmap.

Learn more ↗
07

Reporting & model updates

Keep the interpretation useful as the project evolves.

Explain the findings for technical and nontechnical audiences. Revisit the model when new drilling, surveys or other information becomes available.

You receive: An accessible report, agreed digital deliverables and a record of what changed.

THE REPORT

what we do not know yet.

The findings

A plain-language summary, maps and sections explaining the most important observations and interpretations.

The confidence

Data sources, quality, assumptions, alternative explanations and areas where evidence is limited.

The next decision

Prioritized recommendations and the additional work that would best address the remaining questions.

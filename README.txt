DWELL — PORTABLE HTML WEBSITE

Open index.html in your browser. All website images, styles and scripts are included; no installation or build step is needed.

PAGES
Home, Applications, Services, Methods, Preliminary Tabletop Consultation, Field Development Planning, Data & Supercomputing, Team, Publications, News, Resources & Insights, plus ten detailed technology guides.

EDITING
Edit text directly in the corresponding .html file. Shared presentation is in style.css. The images are in assets/. Keep these files together when copying to your website or hosting service. Links are relative, so the folder can live within an existing website.

IMAGES
Custom illustrations were generated with OpenAI's built-in image generator. These are conceptual illustrations, not site measurements or photographs of completed work. Exact prompts are included in image-prompts.json. Optimized website images are included in assets/.

CONSULTATION WORKSHEET
The consultation form downloads a local text brief. It does not transmit information or book an appointment. Add your verified contact information and connect your chosen booking/contact service before using the site to receive inquiries.

BEFORE PUBLIC BUSINESS LAUNCH
Confirm your direct/partner method delivery scope, founder biography, computing capabilities and service descriptions. Add approved contact details. The current site makes no invented client, project, performance or registration claims.

ONE-FILE EDITION
Dwell-complete.html contains every page section with all images, styles and scripts embedded. Open it directly in a browser. It is convenient for review; the multi-page edition is easier to integrate and maintain.

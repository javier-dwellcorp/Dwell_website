DATA & SUPERCOMPUTING

More information.
A connected interpretation.

A map, a well log and a geophysical survey each tell part of the story. Our work is to understand how those pieces fit—and where they do not.

WHAT SUPERCOMPUTING ADDS

Room to test
more possibilities.

Dwell uses high-performance computing—often called supercomputing—to handle large datasets and demanding three-dimensional modelling tasks. This allows us to examine more detail and compare different explanations for the measurements.

More computing power does not automatically make an interpretation correct. The quality of the inputs, the assumptions in the model and experienced geological judgment remain essential.

Bring data together

Align locations, formats and scales so information from different sources can be compared meaningfully.

Build & test models

Find possible underground arrangements that explain the measurements, then test their sensitivity to assumptions.

Show the confidence

Explain which features are supported by several sources and which need more measurements or direct testing.

FROM MEASUREMENTS TO MEANING

What happens to your data?

  1. Check its origin and quality

    Identify who collected it, where, when, how and under what usage terms.

  2. Prepare it for comparison

    Review noise, coordinates, units and coverage. Record limitations rather than hiding them.

  3. Build a model

    A model is a possible explanation of the measurements—not a photograph of the underground.

  4. Test it against other evidence

    Compare with wells, mapped geology and complementary surveys. Investigate disagreements.

  5. Explain the result

    Deliver maps, sections and a report that separates observations, interpretations and open questions.

DATA ACCESS & BROKERAGE

The missing piece
may already exist.

Before collecting more data, we look at whether public or commercially available information can answer the question. We can help identify, evaluate and arrange the purchase or licensing of suitable datasets.

We distinguish public information, your own records and third-party licensed material. Ownership and permitted use remain explicit throughout the work.

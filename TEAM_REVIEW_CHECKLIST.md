# Dwell Website Prototype Review

Use this checklist during the team review of the homepage and simplified ATLAS demonstration. Record decisions and the person responsible for any follow-up content.

## Homepage story

- [ ] The opening message explains Dwell clearly to someone unfamiliar with the company.
- [ ] First Nations Communities and Industry Partners feel equally intentional, while reflecting their different needs.
- [ ] The sequence from audience need to evidence, capability, technology and action feels natural.
- [ ] The established line “Your land. Your data. Your decisions. Your future.” appears in the right place and tone.

## Brand and experience

- [ ] The logo treatment and colour palette feel consistent with Dwell.
- [ ] The balance of editorial storytelling, land motifs and technical content feels appropriate.
- [ ] Technology feels advanced and understandable without becoming more important than people and place.
- [ ] The homepage remains clear and usable on desktop and mobile.

## Capabilities and claims

- [ ] Dwell Geo, Dwell Digital, Opportunity Assessment and Dwell Learning are described accurately.
- [ ] Community, industry and technology language uses terms Dwell would use publicly.
- [ ] No placeholder statement could be mistaken for a measured client outcome.
- [ ] Data stewardship and partnership language is appropriate for external presentation.

## Client access and ATLAS

- [ ] The Client Login control is easy to find in the header and homepage.
- [ ] `atlas-demo` opens the demonstration without a password.
- [ ] Direct access to `/atlas` requires the demonstration profile.
- [ ] Layer controls, map selection, evidence details and logout work as expected.
- [ ] The demonstration-only status and synthetic nature of the data are clear.
- [ ] The simplified map communicates ATLAS value without implying that it is the complete product.

## Content required for the next round

- [ ] Assign an owner for approved community and industry examples.
- [ ] Assign an owner for ATLAS screenshots, feature claims and public data.
- [ ] Assign an owner for team biographies and photographs.
- [ ] Confirm the project inquiry mailbox and routing responsibility.
- [ ] Confirm privacy, terms, analytics and accessibility review owners.
- [ ] Record permissions for every photograph, partner logo, client name, map and project detail considered for publication.

## Approval decision

- [ ] Approve the direction for expansion to the phase-one pages.
- [ ] Approve with the changes recorded below.
- [ ] Revise the homepage direction before expanding it.

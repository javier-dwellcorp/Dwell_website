THE DWELL TEAM

One integrated team.
From the field to your final Atlas.

Aline leads the technical programme. Javier brings each discipline together in the models and report. Our project, field and specialist team connects that work to the decisions you need to make.

AM
Founder · Technical Director & Integrated Project Lead

Dr. Aline Menezes

Aline leads the technical programme from survey design and fieldwork through processing, modelling, interpretation and final review. Her experience includes MT, AMT and TDEM, Strataimage project work and research links with the University of Alberta and UNISINOS. She brings more than a decade of leadership in advanced geophysical techniques.

Technical direction · Quality review · Integrated interpretation
JM
Project Director & Business Relationship Lead

Jason Michaud

Jason leads customer and community relationships, project delivery, field coordination and economic-development direction. He connects technical findings with land planning, partnerships and future opportunities across First Nation, Métis, private-company and government projects.

Customer relationships · Delivery · Development strategy
JG
Product Integration, Modelling & Reporting Lead

Javier Gonzalez Garcia

Javier supports MT field planning, data conversion, quality control, processing and 3D modelling. He brings specialist contributions into coordinated maps, interpretations, printable reports and the HTML 3D Atlas, while documenting a repeatable project workflow.

Data integration · 3D models · Report & Atlas
MT
Drone, Topography & Field Specialist

Matt Thomas

Matt provides certified drone and topography support for surface mapping and field verification. His work adds terrain context, supports station access planning and connects subsurface interpretation with practical conditions above ground.

Drone mapping · Terrain · Field support
DW
Environmental, Carbon & Funding Consultant

Daniel White

Daniel leads environmental and cumulative-effects review, carbon accounting, stewardship planning and grant-ready narratives. He connects land, water and environmental priorities with the Atlas and translates evidence into practical protection, monitoring and funding actions.

Environment · Carbon · Funding narratives
MM
Senior Oil & Gas & Wellsite Advisor

Marcel Michaud

Marcel brings senior oil-and-gas and wellsite experience to project kickoff, well-data review and assessment after MT acquisition. He helps test geological interpretations against surrounding wells, operating history and practical development conditions.

Wellsite experience · Operating context · Development review

23 January 2026 · IEA

Geothermal investment is growing. Early evidence still matters.

The IEA reports growing financing and commercial interest in next-generation geothermal, alongside continuing upfront risks and a need for supportive policy.

What it means for your planning

For Canadian teams, this reinforces the value of a staged assessment: establish the heat evidence, geological setting and technology requirements before committing to a large field programme. MT can contribute structural context; it does not establish temperature or economics.

2023–2027 programme · Natural Resources Canada

Public geoscience is an asset worth reviewing first.

Canada’s Critical Minerals Geoscience and Data Initiative allocates $69.2 million to Geological Survey of Canada research between April 2023 and March 2027, supporting integrated datasets, knowledge and predictive models.

What it means for your planning

Our practical takeaway: a fresh review of public information can help define the next exploration question. Dwell can inventory relevant records, assess their quality and identify which gaps justify licensed data or new measurements.

16 January 2025 · USGS research

Geologic hydrogen is an emerging screening question.

USGS published a prospectivity assessment for potential geologic hydrogen accumulations in the contiguous United States. It is a research-led screening framework, not a catalogue of proven resources.

What it means for your planning

For Canada, the implication is a question to investigate, not a transferable map of discoveries. Any early assessment would need local geological evidence and direct testing. Electrical or magnetic anomalies alone do not demonstrate hydrogen.
